Statement Fetcher

Statement Fetcher is a small personal tool that saves a person's own bank statements to their own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Once a day it searches the owner's Gmail (read-only) for emails from their bank that contain a PDF statement.
  • It removes the PDF's password, using a password the owner stores as an encrypted secret.
  • It saves the unlocked copy into one folder in the owner's Google Drive, so the statements are in one place for the owner's personal budgeting.

Who it is for

It is used by one person, its developer, on that person's own Google account. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and no other users' data is accessed.

Google permissions it asks for

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): to find and read statement emails and their PDF attachments. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Drive, files it creates only (drive.file): to save the unlocked statements. It cannot see any other file in Drive.
